Help and contact (Android)

Last updated 15 October 2026

A question, a problem, an idea? Write to neousaxis@gmail.com. Never attach your data or your backup: they are not needed to help you.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. FlowMoon has no account and no server, and does not even have permission to access the Internet. Your data stays on your phone, encrypted. The details are in the privacy policy.

How do I switch phones without losing my data?

In Settings, My data, "Export a backup" with a passphrase, then "Import a backup" on the new phone. Google backups and phone transfers do not contain your FlowMoon data: this is on purpose, so that it never leaves your phone without you.

I forgot my backup passphrase

It cannot be recovered: nobody but you knows it, and that is what protects the file. As long as FlowMoon is installed on your phone, your data is still there: you can export a new backup with a new passphrase.

My fingerprint or face is no longer recognized

Your phone's PIN, pattern or password also unlocks FlowMoon.

Are the predictions reliable?

They are estimates calculated from your recent cycles, shown with their uncertainty range. They get more accurate as you log your periods. FlowMoon is neither a method of contraception nor a medical device: for any question about your health, talk to your doctor.

How can I make FlowMoon more discreet?

In Settings, Privacy: lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, discreet notifications (a neutral text that never mentions periods or symptoms) and a discreet icon.

My reminders arrive late

To save battery, Android can delay a reminder by up to an hour. To get them right on time, tap "Allow exact time" in Settings, Reminders, or allow "Alarms & reminders" for FlowMoon in your phone's Settings (Apps, FlowMoon).

How do I erase everything?

In Settings, My data, "Delete everything". It is permanent.
