FlowMoon

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

FlowMoon is an app for tracking your menstrual cycle, perimenopause and menopause. It is designed so that your data stays yours: no account, no server, no tracking.

In short

  • FlowMoon does not collect any data. Its publisher receives nothing: not your periods, not your symptoms, not your first name, not any identifier.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics tools and no third-party software development kits, and it does not connect to any server of the publisher. Only FlowMoon+ purchases go through Apple's App Store.
  • Everything you log stays on your iPhone, encrypted.

What you log, and where it stays

FlowMoon saves what you enter: period dates and flow, symptoms, mood, notes, hot flashes, treatments, answers to the symptom check-in you do each month, reminder settings and, if you provide them, your first name and year of birth (used only on the report for your doctor).

This data is saved only on your iPhone, in a file encrypted with 256-bit AES-GCM. The encryption key is kept in the iPhone Keychain and is never synced with iCloud Keychain.

iPhone backup

If iCloud Backup for your iPhone (or its backup on a computer) is turned on, iOS includes FlowMoon's data in it, like the data of your other apps. This backup is managed by Apple, on your account and according to your settings. FlowMoon's publisher has no access to it.

What leaves your iPhone, only if you decide

  • The PDF report for your doctor is created on your iPhone. It is shared only if you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet.
  • The exported backup is a file you save wherever you want. By default, it is protected by a passphrase you choose (AES-GCM encryption, key derived with PBKDF2). If you turn this protection off, anyone who opens the file can read it.

Purchases in the app (FlowMoon+)

FlowMoon+ is bought in the app, through the App Store. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple, on your Apple account and under Apple's privacy policy. FlowMoon's publisher receives neither your name, nor your email address, nor your payment details, and none of your health data is linked to a purchase.

To know whether FlowMoon+ is unlocked, the app asks your iPhone's purchase system (StoreKit), which may contact the App Store. It also asks whether you downloaded FlowMoon while it was free: if so, FlowMoon+ is yours free for life and the app notes this in your iPhone's Keychain. Nothing else is saved.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled by iOS on your iPhone. No server sends them. In discreet mode, they only show "FlowMoon" and a neutral text.

Face ID and passcode

If you turn on the lock, the check is done by iOS. FlowMoon only receives the answer (accepted or refused), never your face or your passcode.

Health data

Your health data is never used for advertising, profiling or research, and is not shared with anyone. FlowMoon does not use Apple's Health app (HealthKit).

FlowMoon is neither a method of contraception nor a medical device. Predictions are estimates. For any question about your health, talk to your doctor.

Deleting your data

In Settings, My data, "Delete everything" erases the encrypted file, its key and the scheduled reminders. Deleting the app also erases its data from the iPhone. iPhone backups already made follow Apple's retention rules.

Your rights

The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) give you the right to access, correct and erase your data. Since the publisher holds no data about you, you exercise these rights directly in the app: everything there can be viewed, changed, exported and deleted.

Children

FlowMoon does not collect any data, so no data about children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and in the app, with its date.
