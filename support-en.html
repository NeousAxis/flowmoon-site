Help and contact

Last updated 14 November 2026

A question, a problem, an idea? Write to neousaxis@gmail.com. Never attach your data or your backup: they are not needed to help you.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. FlowMoon has no account and no server. Your data stays on your iPhone, encrypted. The details are in the privacy policy.

I had FlowMoon before it became paid: do I have to pay?

No. Everyone who downloaded FlowMoon while it was free keeps FlowMoon+ for life, without paying anything. If the app still offers you FlowMoon+, tap "Restore purchases" in Settings, FlowMoon+, with the Apple account that downloaded FlowMoon. If that is not enough, write to us.

How do I switch iPhones without losing my data?

The safest way: in Settings, My data, "Export a backup" with a passphrase, then "Import a backup" on the new iPhone. Restoring an iCloud backup also works in most cases.

I forgot my backup passphrase

It cannot be recovered: nobody but you knows it, and that is what protects the file. As long as FlowMoon is installed on your iPhone, your data is still there: you can export a new backup with a new passphrase.

Face ID no longer recognizes me

Your iPhone passcode also unlocks FlowMoon.

Are the predictions reliable?

They are estimates calculated from your recent cycles, shown with their uncertainty range. They get more accurate as you log your periods. FlowMoon is neither a method of contraception nor a medical device: for any question about your health, talk to your doctor.

How can I make FlowMoon more discreet?

In Settings, Privacy: lock with Face ID or passcode, discreet notifications (a neutral text that never mentions periods or symptoms) and a discreet icon.

How do I erase everything?

In Settings, My data, "Delete everything". It is permanent.
