FlowMoon

Privacy Policy (Android)

Last updated 15 October 2026

FlowMoon is an app for tracking your menstrual cycle, perimenopause and menopause. It is designed so that your data stays yours: no account, no server, no tracking.

In short

  • FlowMoon does not collect any data. Its publisher receives nothing: not your periods, not your symptoms, not your first name, not any identifier.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics tools and no third-party software development kits. On Android, it does not even have permission to access the Internet.
  • Everything you log stays on your phone, encrypted.

What you log, and where it stays

FlowMoon saves what you enter: period dates and flow, symptoms, mood, notes, hot flashes, treatments, answers to the symptom check-in you do each month, reminder settings and, if you provide them, your first name and year of birth (used only on the report for your doctor).

This data is saved only on your phone, in a file encrypted with 256-bit AES-GCM. The encryption key is protected by the Android Keystore and never leaves the phone.

Backups

FlowMoon is excluded from Google backups and from transfers to a new phone: its data does not leave your phone that way. To switch phones, use "Export a backup" in the app.

What leaves your phone, only if you decide

  • The PDF report for your doctor is created on your phone. It is shared only if you choose a destination in the Android share menu.
  • The exported backup is a file you save wherever you want. By default, it is protected by a passphrase you choose (AES-GCM encryption, key derived with PBKDF2). If you turn this protection off, anyone who opens the file can read it.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled by Android on your phone. No server sends them. In discreet mode, they only show "FlowMoon" and a neutral text.

Fingerprint, face and screen lock

If you turn on the lock, the check is done by Android. FlowMoon only receives the answer (accepted or refused), never your fingerprint, your face or your screen lock code.

Health data

Your health data is never used for advertising, profiling or research, and is not shared with anyone. FlowMoon does not use Health Connect.

FlowMoon is neither a method of contraception nor a medical device. Predictions are estimates. For any question about your health, talk to your doctor.

Deleting your data

In Settings, My data, "Delete everything" erases the encrypted file, its key and the scheduled reminders. Uninstalling the app also erases all its data from the phone.

Your rights

The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) give you the right to access, correct and erase your data. Since the publisher holds no data about you, you exercise these rights directly in the app: everything there can be viewed, changed, exported and deleted.

Children

FlowMoon does not collect any data, so no data about children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and in the app, with its date.
